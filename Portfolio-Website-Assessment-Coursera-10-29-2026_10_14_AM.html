About

Structure, style, and behaviour — each in its place.

I write semantic HTML for structure, CSS for presentation, and JavaScript for interaction — keeping the three clearly separated so pages stay easy to read, maintain, and extend.

My work is responsive by default: flexible layouts and media queries mean nothing gets cut off, whether the page is opened on a 320 px phone or a wide desktop monitor. I also care about spacing and consistent typography, because readable pages are usable pages.
